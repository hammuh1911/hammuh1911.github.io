About Me

Welcome. My name is Anwar Malik.

I am a lawyer with interests in law, economics, philosophy, computer science, artificial intelligence, and institutional design. This website serves as a central location for my professional background, research interests, and ongoing technical projects.

Much of my current work explores how mathematical models, economics, and artificial intelligence can be applied to legal decision-making. My goal is to develop transparent analytical frameworks that assist attorneys in evaluating litigation strategy while remaining understandable and practically useful.

Research Interests

  • Law and Economics
  • Game Theory
  • Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models
  • Civil Litigation Valuation
  • Institutional Design
  • Political Philosophy
  • Decision Theory
  • Legal Technology

Current Projects

  • Adaptive Litigation Valuation Framework

    A modular legal-economic framework that progresses from simple expected-value analysis to dynamic stochastic game theory as additional litigation information becomes available.

  • Research into practical applications of large language models for legal analysis and decision support.

Website Philosophy

This website emphasizes simplicity, transparency, and open educational resources. Wherever practical, research models and analytical tools are made available with complete mathematical specifications and reusable prompts that can be used with modern large language models.
